# Download archive pop-up blocks its controls while queuing and closes only once the archive is queued

Test verifies that:
- clicking **OK** in the **Download archive** pop-up does not close it right away
- while the archive-download task is being queued, **OK** shows a loading spinner and, together with the **Archive name** field, **Cancel** and the close cross, is blocked - a click on the mask outside the pop-up and the **Esc** key have no effect either
- the pop-up closes only once the task has actually been queued

**Prerequisites**:
- A user with **READ** and **WRITE** permissions on an S3 object storage (or an NFS storage whose share is mounted as NFS)

**Preparations**:
1. Login as the user from the Prerequisites
2. Open the storage from the Prerequisites
3. Upload or create at least one file

| Steps | Actions | Expected results |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Select the file from Preparations via its checkbox | |
| 2 | Click the selection button (**"1 item selected"**) and choose **Download archive** | The **Download archive** pop-up opens. The **Archive name** field is pre-filled with a default name. **OK** is enabled |
| 3 | Click **OK** | <li> **OK** shows a loading spinner and becomes disabled <li> the **Archive name** field becomes disabled <li> **Cancel** becomes disabled <li> clicking the close cross, clicking the mask outside the pop-up, and pressing **Esc** all have no effect - the pop-up stays open and the spinner keeps showing |
| 4 | Wait for the archive-download task to be queued | The pop-up closes on its own. The notification `Archive download has been queued. Follow its progress under "Show downloads".` appears |

**After**:
- Cancel the queued task from the **Downloads** window (click the grid-icon button at the top right of the storage view, choose **Show downloads**, then **Cancel** the task), or let it finish and discard the downloaded archive
